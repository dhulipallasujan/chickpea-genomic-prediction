# Results files

Generated by `Rscript run_genomic_prediction.R`.

- `results_table.md` - cross-validation, leave-one-season-out and marker-density tables (also written into the root README).
- `multienvironment_validation_summary.csv`, `..._repeats.csv`, `..._folds.csv` - grouped CV1 and CV2 metrics, per repeat and per fold (selected penalties included).
- `leave_one_season_out.csv`, `marker_density_sensitivity*.csv`, `multienvironment_qc.csv` - year transfer, density sensitivity, and QC including the share of tuned penalties at a grid edge and the season-to-season yield correlation.
- `rrblup_crosscheck.csv` - hand-written ridge versus `rrBLUP::mixed.solve` on one CV1 repeat.
- `genomic_prediction_*.csv`, `marker_pca_variance.csv` - one-season benchmark and population-structure QC.
- `multienvironment_validation_predictions.csv.gz` - out-of-fold predictions (large; not tracked by git).

SD columns describe variation across repeated random splits, not confidence intervals over chickpea populations. The two seasons are at one location, so leave-one-season-out is not independent-location validation. Results are exploratory.
